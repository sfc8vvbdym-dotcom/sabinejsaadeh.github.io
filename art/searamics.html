Joseph Saadeh (Jozef S)
Year 2013
Medium Acrylic on canvas
Dimensions 120 × 87 × 3 cm
Artwork Original painting
Series Searamics

About the work

Searamics draws on Joseph Saadeh’s childhood memories of Ghana, where the sea represented both escape and freedom.

The work brings together two of Saadeh’s enduring fascinations: the human form and the quiet, organic presence of ceramic vessels. To him, ceramic jars possess an almost human quality. Although shaped by human hands, their rounded bodies, imperfections and natural silhouettes can appear strangely alive.

Here, the vessels are imagined as two lovers resting together on a Ghanaian-inspired coastline, turned towards the sea and towards one another.

A withered tree trunk interrupts the scene, evoking the debris and human waste that increasingly find their way into coastal landscapes. The work therefore holds two ideas at once: the sea as a place of memory, intimacy and escape, and the fragile environment that surrounds it.

Searamics is a meditation on the relationship between the human, the natural and the handmade—where vessels become figures, landscape becomes memory, and the sea becomes both refuge and witness.
