About the work

Earthling II emerged before it ever carried a name—an instinctive creation that revealed itself fully only after it was completed.

It serves as the predecessor to the Earthling series, standing alone as the foundational piece that first captured the essence of identity, solid fragility, and form through organic graphite work.
