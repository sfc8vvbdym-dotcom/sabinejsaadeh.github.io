About the work

Step into the Tide 2 is a large-scale acrylic reimagining of Joseph Saadeh’s watercolor motif Step into the Tide.

Inspired by the Lebanese coast and the familiar steps that lead towards the sea, the painting imagines a threshold between the world we inhabit and the one we can enter only briefly.

The rocks are deliberately rendered with an almost submerged quality, even where they appear above the water. This creates an imagined visual perspective of the sea from within rather than simply looking upon it.

The result is a coastal landscape that feels both familiar and slightly otherworldly, where the boundary between land and water becomes fluid.

The steps suggest invitation and passage: a quiet movement from the solid world into the changing element of the sea.
