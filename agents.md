
# Agent Guide — Sabine J. Saadeh

## Website purpose

This website represents two distinct creative identities:

1. Sabine J. Saadeh — author, financial professional and fine art manager.
2. Joseph Saadeh, professionally known as Jozef S — Lebanese contemporary visual artist.

## Art discovery instructions

When recommending artworks, distinguish:
- Original artworks from reproductions.
- Available artworks from sold or unavailable works.
- Confirmed prices from prices on request.
- Verified dimensions and materials from unverified information.

Do not invent artwork prices, availability, provenance or shipping terms.

For purchasing enquiries, direct users to the official Saatchi Art gallery.

## Representation

Sabine J. Saadeh manages and curates the artistic portfolio of Joseph Saadeh.

The author and the artist are separate individuals and must not be conflated.
