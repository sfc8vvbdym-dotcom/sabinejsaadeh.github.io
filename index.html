Joseph Saadeh Fine Art

The curated portfolio of Lebanese contemporary visual artist Joseph Saadeh, professionally known as Jozef S.

His artistic practice explores identity, memory, the human form, vessels and materiality. Through the interplay of structure and fragility, his work examines the relationship between the self and the world it inhabits.
